Privacy policy

Last updated 7 November 2026. Covers Look Up version 1.3 and later.

This policy covers apps published by Potential Realty LLC ("PRL").

Look Up

What PRL receives: nothing. Look Up has no accounts and we run no servers, advertising or analytics services for it, so we never receive your searches or anything else you do in the app.

What stays on your phone: the Bible text and dictionary are packaged inside the app. Look Up saves your search history on your phone so you can go back to earlier lookups. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in your phone's settings, removes it.

Android backup: from version 1.7, Look Up turns off Android's app backup for its data, so your search history, journal and highlights are not copied into any cloud backup. They live only in the app on your phone. Earlier versions followed your phone's backup settings.

Recovery Waypoints

What PRL receives: nothing. Recovery Waypoints has no accounts and we run no servers, advertising or analytics services for it, so we never receive your clean date, your meeting log, your searches or anything else you do in the app.

What stays on your phone: the meeting list is packaged inside the app and searches run on the phone. Your clean date, meeting log and settings are saved in the app's storage on your phone only, and Android backup is turned off for the app's data, so they are not copied into any cloud backup. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in your phone's settings, removes them.

The one time the app uses the internet: the resource map loads its map library from unpkg and its map pictures (tiles) from OpenStreetMap when you open it. Those services see the request your phone has to make to fetch any picture from the internet — its internet (IP) address and browser type, and roughly which area's map is on screen. They do not receive anything you typed or recorded in the app, and neither do we. Links you tap (for example na.org or a treatment centre's own site) open in your browser like any website, under that site's own privacy policy.

Children

Our apps are not directed at children under 13 and do not knowingly collect information from anyone.
