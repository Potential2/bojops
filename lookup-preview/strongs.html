Look first — camera

Snap it, then search it

Point the camera at a page, a sign, a plate — anything carrying a Strong’s number or a word you want. Take as many photos as you like; nothing is read at capture time. A photo is read only after you say it is good enough, and the reading happens on this device. Photos snapped here are never kept: once the words are read, the picture is discarded, and it never goes to your gallery.

⚑ TEXT READING NOT LIVE — the on-device reader isn’t available on this surface, so photos can be taken and reviewed but not read here yet.

A plain number shows both languages, labelled Hebrew / Greek. A word (love, grace, punish) shows Hebrew and Greek side by side — tap any row for the full entry. Use H or G in front of a number to stay in one language.

Search the words of the Bible itself. Two or more words are matched as an exact phrase; one word matches whole words only. Tap any verse to open it in the Reader. The whole Bible is inside the app — this works fully offline.

Strong's definitions — OpenScriptures Strong's Hebrew and Greek lexicons (CC BY-SA) — Hebrew e-text by David Instone-Brewer with edits by David Troidl; Greek XML by Ulrik Petersen. In this build the definitions are complete: the first half that earlier builds dropped was restored on 2026-10-24 from the same source (council work refs DEF-001 / DEF-002).

Bible text — King James Version, CrossWire SWORD KJV module v3.1. The KJV text is public domain in the United States (Crown rights apply in the UK); the CrossWire module is distributed under the GPL. Strong's tagging: The Bible Foundation (OT) and the KJV2003 Project (NT). Cross-references: OpenBible.info cross-reference data, used under CC BY.

Word index — built 2026-10-24 from this project's verified Strong's + KJV packs (L-013 / L-014), so every English word points at the Hebrew and Greek words the KJV renders with it.

The app — Look Up v1.10.6, built by Peet. Fully offline: no account, no tracking, nothing leaves the phone.

History

Every search and every entry you open, kept on this device only — newest first, grouped by day. Tap a search to run it again, or an entry to open it.

Journal

Your own notes, saved on this device only. Journal entries never leave the phone.

Journal photos are taking up room — tap any entry to remove its photo.

Off unless you turn it on. Turning it on asks Android for location permission — you can say no. When it is on, a new entry saves the coordinates of where you are, on this device only; nothing is sent anywhere. Entries already saved are never changed.

Export and backup save the whole journal as one JSON file — entries with their dates, places, links and uncertainty flags. Photos themselves stay in the app's photo store on this device. Restore asks first: replace the journal here, or merge and keep what is here.
